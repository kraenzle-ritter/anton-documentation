# Archive description (ISDIAH)

ISDIAH — *International Standard for Describing Institutions with Archival
Holdings* — describes not the holdings but the **archive itself**: address,
opening hours, conditions of access, history, services.

If the description is set up, the entry **About us** appears in the navigation.
It is maintained on the admin page under **Settings**; the `admin` role is
sufficient.

## What it is for

The information is aimed at outsiders who want to know where they stand before
visiting — when it is open, whether an appointment is necessary, what the
archive holds at all. Because ISDIAH is a standard, the information can also be
evaluated automatically and passed on to portals.

## Relationship to the other standards

Anton implements four standards that complement one another:

| Standard | Describes |
|---|---|
| ISAD(G) | the [units of description](../user/objects.md) |
| ISAAR(CPF) | the [actors](../user/actors.md) |
| ISDIAH | the institution |
| RiC-O, CIDOC CRM | the [output as linked data](download-rdf.md) |

The areas of information of the first three can be read in the original text in
the application under **Help → Standards**.

## Optional

ISDIAH is not a prerequisite for operation. Without a description, the «About
us» entry is simply missing — everything else works unchanged.
