# Home page and navigation

The home page and parts of the navigation can be freely designed per archive.
They are maintained on the admin page under **Settings**.

## What can be adapted

| Component | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Navigation title** | The menu entry for the catalogue. It carries the name the archive chooses — «catalogue», «archive plan» or the name of the archive. Without a value, no entry appears. |
| **Additional navigation** | A freely designable block for further menu entries |
| **Home page text** | The text on the home page |
| **Title of the root entry** | The heading above the archival arrangement |

Added to this are [logo and favicons](logo.md).

## What does not appear in the menu by itself

Anton comes with some areas but does **not** link to them of its own accord.
Anyone wishing to offer them adds them via the additional navigation:

- the [media gallery](gallery.md) at `/gallery`
- the [documents](documents.md) at `/documents`
- the [correspondences](../user/correspondences.md) at `/correspondences`

!!! warning "Not linked does not mean protected"
    A missing menu entry does not hide an area — the address is reachable for
    anyone who knows it. What is not meant to be public belongs behind
    [protection periods](protection-periods.md) and the «blocked» marker, not
    behind the omission of a link.

## Multilingualism

The texts can be stored per language. Which languages are available is
controlled by the archive's language setting — see
[language configuration](../user/languages.md).
